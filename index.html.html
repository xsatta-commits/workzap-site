FAQ

Questions we hear every day.

How does the per-department pricing work?

Each department costs PKR 1M / $3,500 and takes 30–90 days to fully automate. You start with one department — your biggest problem — and add more whenever you're ready. There's no obligation to buy more than one.

What happens to my Google Sheets data?

We migrate all your existing data into the new system. Every record, every history, every entry comes across cleanly. You can also keep using Sheets during the build if needed — we'll sync everything at go-live.

Do I own the system after it's built?

Your data is 100% yours and fully exportable at any time. The system runs on our secure infrastructure — like any SaaS product — which we maintain, secure, and update for you monthly for a retainer fee.

How many of my staff will use the system?

Most of our clients have between 3–10 active system users, regardless of total company size. A garment factory with 300 workers typically has the owner, finance manager, production manager, and a few supervisors using the system. We price by department, not by user count.

Do you work with businesses outside Pakistan?

Yes — we actively serve clients in UAE, Saudi Arabia, and the UK. Our systems handle multi-currency, multi-timezone, and can be built in English or Urdu depending on your team's preference.

What if we need more departments later?

That's the whole idea. Start with Core at PKR 1M for your most painful department. When you see it working, add the next one. Each addition is another PKR 1M and 30–90 days. Many clients start with finance and gradually build to a full Command-level system over 12–18 months.
